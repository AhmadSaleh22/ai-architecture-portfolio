My work for the course. The session notes and assignments use the course's example system, ShopCo. My capstone is a different system, Hesal, in [capstone/](capstone/).

## Scenario

An online store whose marketing is all manual: fixed offers on the home page, discounts added by hand, campaigns sent from the admin panel. The target adds AI around the existing stores: customer segments, next-best action for abandoned carts and likely churners, and campaigns prepared by agents. Business goal: CSAT from 60% to 80%.
