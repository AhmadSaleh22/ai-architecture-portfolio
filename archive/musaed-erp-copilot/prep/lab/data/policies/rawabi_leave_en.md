## Annual leave
Rawabi Retail employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per year. Store staff must book leave at least 14 days in advance and leave is blocked during Ramadan and Eid peak season.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year. Remaining days are paid out at basic salary rate.
