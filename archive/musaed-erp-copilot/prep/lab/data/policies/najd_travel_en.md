## Daily allowance
Najd employees travelling for business inside Saudi Arabia receive a per diem of SAR 450 per day. International travel per diem is SAR 800 per day.

## Approval
All business travel must be approved by the line manager at least 5 working days before departure. Trips longer than 10 days also need approval from the finance director.
