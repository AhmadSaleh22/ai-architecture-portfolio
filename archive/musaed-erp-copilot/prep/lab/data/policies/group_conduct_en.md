## Confidentiality of salary data
Salary, bank account and national ID information is strictly confidential. Employees may only view their own payroll data. HR staff may view payroll data only for the company they are assigned to.

## Reporting concerns
Employees can report concerns anonymously through the group ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.
